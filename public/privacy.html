Rent Coat Co. is a small clothing business based in North Carolina. This policy explains what information we collect on rentcoat.co, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect only what it takes to sell you a shirt and tell you about drops, we never sell it, and this site doesn't track you.

What we collect

WhenWhatWho handles it
You place an orderName, email, shipping and billing address, what you bought, payment details, and how you found us (one dropdown question, so we know which posts and ads work)Stripe collects it on its checkout page. We see your order and address, never your full card number.
You sign up for drop remindersYour email, the time you signed up, and the IP address and browser info recorded with the formNetlify, our website host, stores the list.
You email or message usWhatever you choose to sendGmail, or Instagram and TikTok if you message us there
You visit the siteStandard technical data: IP address, browser type, pages requested, and the timeNetlify keeps short-term server logs to run and protect the site. Our fonts are hosted on rentcoat.co, so no other company is contacted when a page loads.

We don't use ad pixels, third-party analytics or tracking tools, and we don't collect information from other sources about you. Our own site keeps daily counts of page views: which page, the site that linked you there (if any), and whether it was a phone or a computer. Those counts don't use cookies and don't include anything that identifies you (how we count visits). We don't ask for sensitive information like your birthday, Social Security number, or religious affiliation, and we don't use your information to make automated decisions about you.

How we use it

  • To take payment, print your shirt, ship it, and email you about your order.
  • To answer your questions and handle replacements.
  • To email you when a drop opens, only if you signed up for reminders.
  • To prevent fraud, keep the site working, and meet tax and legal requirements.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the companies that help us run the shop, and only what each one needs:

  • Stripe processes payments and sales tax (Stripe's privacy policy).
  • Printful prints and ships your order, so it gets your name, shipping address, email and items (Printful's privacy policy).
  • Netlify hosts the website and stores the reminder list (Netlify's privacy policy).
  • Google provides our email, Gmail (Google's privacy policy).

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or someone's safety, or with a buyer if the business is ever sold. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising. Because of that, there's nothing to opt out of, and we treat a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal as already honored.

Cookies and browser storage

rentcoat.co doesn't use cookies: no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, which is why you don't see a cookie banner. The only thing saved in your browser is your bag (in local storage, as rc-bag), and it stays on your device until you check out. Stripe's checkout page and the sites we link to use their own cookies. Our cookie policy has the full details and how to clear it.

Drop reminder emails

If you sign up, we only email you about drops. Every email says why you're getting it and includes our postal address. To stop them, reply "stop" or email us, and we'll remove you right away (always within 10 business days). Order emails, like receipts and shipping updates, still come while you have an open order.

How long we keep it

  • Orders: as long as tax and accounting rules require, generally 3 to 7 years.
  • Reminder list: until you unsubscribe or we stop doing drops.
  • Emails and messages: as long as we need them to help you.

Security

The site uses HTTPS, card details go straight to Stripe, and only the people who run Rent Coat Co. can see order details. No method is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Kids and teens

rentcoat.co is meant for teens and adults. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you're under 13, please don't send us your email. If you're a parent and think your child under 13 gave us information, email us and we'll delete it. To place an order, you must be 18 or have a parent or guardian's permission.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal information we have about you
  • Correct it
  • Delete it, except what we must keep for tax records or an open order
  • Take you off the reminder list

Email rentcoat.co@gmail.com from the address we have on file. We'll answer within 30 days, and we'll never treat you differently for asking. Some states give you more rights; we'll honor them. If we turn down a request, we'll tell you why, and you can reply to ask us to look at it again.

You can also have someone you trust, like a parent, make a request for you. We'll check with you that you gave them permission before we share or delete anything.

Where we operate

We're based in the United States and ship only to US addresses. Your information is stored and processed in the US.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new date at the top. If a change affects how we use information we already have, we'll ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

Contact

Questions about privacy: email rentcoat.co@gmail.com or message @rentcoat.co on Instagram. Rent Coat Co. is based in North Carolina, USA.
