Contact

Questions about an order? Email rentcoat.co@gmail.com with the name and email on your order, and we'll answer within 2 business days. You can also message @rentcoat.co on Instagram.

Preorders

Each drop is a 7-day preorder, and checkout is closed on Sundays. You pay when you order, and every shirt is printed for you after the drop closes. Prices are in US dollars: $36 per tee, $39 for 2XL. Sales tax is added at checkout where the law requires it.

Shipping

  • We ship to US addresses only, for a flat $5.99 per order.
  • Shirts usually arrive 2 to 3 weeks after the drop closes. For Drop 001 (closes Sat, Nov 21, 2026), we expect every order to ship by Tue, Dec 8, 2026.
  • You'll get tracking by email when your order ships.
  • If we can't ship by the date we gave, we'll email you before then with a new date, and you can cancel for a full refund.

Changes and cancellations

Until the drop closes, email us to change your size or address, or to cancel for a full refund. After it closes, your shirt goes to print and the order can't be changed or cancelled, though we can still update the address until it ships.

Returns and replacements

Because every shirt is made to order, we can't take returns or exchanges for fit or a change of mind. Check the fit notes before you order: the tees are unisex and true to size.

If your shirt arrives damaged, misprinted, or isn't what you ordered, email a photo to rentcoat.co@gmail.com within 30 days of delivery and we'll replace it free, or refund you if we can't.

Lost or returned packages

If tracking shows your package lost, or it hasn't arrived within 30 days of the expected date, email us and we'll replace it. If a package comes back to us because the address was entered wrong, we'll reship it once you pay the shipping again.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the card or wallet you paid with. They usually show up within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to shop Rent Coat Co. We aim to meet the WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines, so the site works with a keyboard, screen readers and zoom. If anything on the site is hard to use, email rentcoat.co@gmail.com. We'll help you order another way and fix the problem.

Privacy

We collect only what it takes to fill your order and send drop reminders, and we never sell it. The site uses no cookies or trackers. Read the full privacy policy, cookie policy and terms of sale.

About

Rent Coat Co. is an independent clothing brand based in North Carolina. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Read the story behind the name.
