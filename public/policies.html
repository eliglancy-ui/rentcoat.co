Rent Coat Co.

Shipping, returns & privacy

Contact

Questions about an order? Email rentcoat.co@gmail.com with the name and email on your order. You can also message @rentcoat.co on Instagram.

Preorders

Each drop is a 7-day preorder. You pay when you order, and every shirt is printed for you after the drop closes. Prices are in US dollars: $36 per tee, $39 for 2XL.

Shipping

We ship to US addresses only, for a flat $5.99 per order. Shirts usually arrive 2 to 3 weeks after the drop closes, and you'll get tracking by email when yours ships.

Returns and replacements

Because every shirt is made to order, we can't take returns or exchanges for fit or a change of mind. Check the fit notes before you order: the tees are unisex and true to size.

If your shirt arrives damaged, misprinted, or isn't what you ordered, email a photo to rentcoat.co@gmail.com within 30 days of delivery and we'll replace it free.

Privacy

  • What we collect: when you order, Stripe collects your name, email, shipping address and payment details. We never see or store your card number.
  • Why: to charge you, print and ship your order, and email you about it. Your name and address go to Printful, the company that prints and ships the shirts.
  • Drop reminders: if you give us your email on the home page, we use it only to tell you when a drop opens. Our website host, Netlify, stores the list. Email us, or reply to any reminder, to be taken off it.
  • What we don't do: we don't sell your information, and this site doesn't use ads or tracking cookies. Your bag is saved only in your own browser.
  • Your choice: email us to see or delete the order details we hold, except what we must keep for tax records.

Payments are handled by Stripe and printing by Printful, under their own privacy policies.
