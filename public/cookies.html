Last updated October 23, 2026

The short version: rentcoat.co doesn't set any cookies. No analytics, no ad pixels, no tracking. The only thing we save in your browser is your shopping bag, and it stays on your device.

What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. "Local storage" is a similar space in your browser that a site can use to remember things on your device. Some sites use these to track you across the web. We don't.

What rentcoat.co stores

NameTypeWhyHow long
rc-bagLocal storage on your device (not a cookie)Remembers the shirts and sizes in your bag so they're still there when you come back. Strictly necessary for the bag to work.Until you empty the bag, finish checking out, or clear this site's data

Your bag never leaves your device until you click Checkout. Then the shirts and sizes (not your name or anything else) are sent to our server to open your Stripe checkout page. Because nothing here tracks you or is used for advertising, there's nothing to accept, and that's why you won't see a cookie banner.

Our fonts are hosted on rentcoat.co itself, so loading a page doesn't contact Google or any other company. Netlify, our web host, keeps short-term server logs (like your IP address and the page you asked for) to run and protect the site. Those logs don't use cookies. Read more in our privacy policy.

When you leave rentcoat.co

Some links take you to other companies' sites, which use their own cookies under their own policies:

  • Checkout: when you click Checkout, you go to Stripe's secure payment page. Stripe uses cookies there to process your payment and prevent fraud (Stripe's cookie policy).
  • Instagram and TikTok: if you follow our links to @rentcoat.co (Instagram's cookie policy, TikTok's cookie policy).
  • Scripture links: the references on our shirts link to churchofjesuschrist.org, which explains its cookies in its own privacy notice.

How to clear it or block it

  • Empty your bag on the site, or finish an order, and rc-bag is cleared.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser settings (usually under Privacy, then Site data) to remove it completely.
  • Block storage for the site if you want. The bag won't remember your shirts between visits, but everything else still works.

We honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals. Since we don't track you, they're already respected.

If this ever changes

If we ever add analytics, advertising, or any other cookies that aren't strictly necessary, we'll update this page first and ask for your permission before setting them where the law requires it.
