Meet the founder

Hi, I'm Eli.

I'm Eli Glancy, a senior in high school in Holly Springs, North Carolina. I started Rent Coat Co. to make clothes that say something about my faith and still look good.

Why I started it

It started with Captain Moroni

Captain Moroni is the reason this brand exists. I'm inspired by his courage, and by how he kept going for his people and never gave up on them. The Book of Mormon calls him “a man who did labor exceedingly for the welfare and safety of his people” (Alma 48:12).

I also wanted clothes that represent my faith and still look good. Not just a verse printed on a shirt, but shirts I'd actually pick to wear on a normal day.

So I started making them. Every design starts with a verse.

Why “Rent Coat”

My favorite story is the title of liberty

My favorite story in the Book of Mormon is the title of liberty. When his people's freedom was in danger, Moroni tore his coat, wrote on a piece of it, and fastened it to the end of a pole. He called it the title of liberty (Alma 46:12–13). Then he went out among the people waving it in the air, so everyone could see what he had written (Alma 46:19).

“Rent” is an old word for torn. That torn coat is where the name Rent Coat comes from.
