How a drop works

Seven days. That's it.

  1. Preorder in the 7-day window

    Pick your shirt and size while the clock is running. When it closes, that design is done.

  2. We print yours

    Every shirt is printed after you order, so nothing gets wasted and nothing sits in a warehouse.

The name

Why a torn coat?

When Captain Moroni needed his people to remember what they were fighting for, he tore his coat, wrote on it, and raised it on a pole. He called it the title of liberty.

Rent Coat Co. is named for that moment: take what you have and turn it into something you stand behind. We make shirts that look like a real streetwear drop and still say what you believe.

“He rent his coat.”

Alma 46:12

FAQ

What is Rent Coat Co.?

An independent clothing brand that makes heavyweight graphic tees for young Latter-day Saints. Every design starts with a scripture reference, printed on the shirt so anyone who asks can look it up.

Is this an LDS clothing brand?

It's made by a member for members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. It's independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Church.

How does the 7-day preorder work?

Each drop is open for exactly 7 days. Shirts are printed after the window closes and usually arrive 2 to 3 weeks after that. After the window closes, that drop is gone.

How do I pay?

Checkout runs on Stripe, so you can pay with any major card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never touch our site. Tees are $36 ($39 for 2XL), and shipping is a flat $5.99 in the US.

What's a curelom?

Nobody knows. Ether 9:19 lists cureloms and cumoms with elephants as animals that were useful to the Jaredites, and that's all the text says. Hence the Wildlife Society.

How do the shirts fit?

Unisex and true to size with a relaxed cut, S to 2XL. Size up one for a boxy fit.

Where does the name come from?

From Alma 46:12–13 in the Book of Mormon. Captain Moroni “rent his coat,” wrote on it, fastened it to a pole, and called it the title of liberty.

Can I return or exchange?

Because every shirt is printed for you, we can't take fit returns. If your shirt arrives damaged or misprinted, send a photo to @rentcoat.co on Instagram within 30 days of delivery and we'll replace it free. Full policies.
