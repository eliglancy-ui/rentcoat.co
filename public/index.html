The name

Why a torn coat?

When Captain Moroni needed his people to remember what they were fighting for, he tore his coat, wrote on it, and raised it on a pole. He called it the title of liberty.

Rent Coat Co. is named for that moment: take what you have and turn it into something you stand behind. We make shirts that look like a real streetwear drop and still say what you believe.

FAQ

Something not here? Email rentcoat.co@gmail.com.

What is Rent Coat Co.?

An independent clothing brand that makes heavyweight graphic tees for young Latter-day Saints. Every design starts with a scripture reference, printed on the shirt so anyone who asks can look it up.

Is this an LDS clothing brand?

It's made by a member for members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. It's independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Church.

How does launch week work?

Drop 001 launches with a 7-day launch week, Nov 14 to Nov 21 (10 AM Eastern both days), when every tee is $35 ($38 for 2XL). Launch week orders are printed after it ends and usually arrive 2 to 3 weeks later, and every one ships by Tue, Dec 8. After that the shirts stay in the shop at $40, printed to order and usually delivered in 1 to 2 weeks.

Why can't I order on Sunday?

Checkout pauses on Sundays (Eastern time), because we keep Sunday as a day of rest. Your bag stays saved, so you can check out on Monday.

How do I pay?

Checkout runs on Stripe, so you can pay with any major card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never touch our site. Tees are $35 during launch week and $40 after ($38 and $43 for 2XL), and shipping in the US is $5.99, or free on orders of $60 or more.

What's a curelom?

Nobody knows. Ether 9:19 lists cureloms and cumoms with elephants as animals that were useful to the Jaredites, and that's all the text says. Hence the Wildlife Society.

How do the shirts fit?

Boxy and oversized, with dropped shoulders and a slightly shorter body. Sizes S to 2XL. Order your usual size for the boxy look, or size down for a closer fit. A medium is about 24¾ inches across the chest and 27¼ inches long.

Where does the name come from?

From Alma 46:12–13 in the Book of Mormon. Captain Moroni “rent his coat,” wrote on it, fastened it to a pole, and called it the title of liberty.

Can I return or exchange?

Because every shirt is printed for you, we can't take fit returns. If your shirt arrives damaged or misprinted, email a photo to rentcoat.co@gmail.com within 30 days of delivery and we'll replace it free. Shipping and returns.
