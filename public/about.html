About Rent Coat Co.

Why a torn coat?

Rent Coat Co. is an independent clothing brand that makes heavyweight graphic tees for young Latter-day Saints. Every design starts with a Book of Mormon reference, and the reference is printed right on the shirt so anyone who asks can look it up.

The name

A coat, a pole, and a reminder

In Alma 46 in the Book of Mormon, the Nephites are close to losing their freedom. Captain Moroni, the leader of their armies, tears his own coat, writes a reminder of what they're defending on one piece, and fastens it to the end of a pole. He calls it the title of liberty, and he goes out among the people waving it so everyone can see what he wrote.

"Rent" is the old word for torn. Rent Coat Co. is named for that moment: take what you have and turn it into something you stand behind.

The flag in our logo is the title of liberty. We make shirts that look like a real streetwear drop and still say what you believe.

How it works

No warehouse. No waste.

We don't keep a warehouse full of shirts. Every shirt is printed after you order, so nothing goes to waste.

New designs launch with a 7-day launch week at a lower price. Launch week orders are printed together after it ends and arrive about 2 to 3 weeks later. After that the designs stay in the shop, and orders usually arrive in 1 to 2 weeks, with tracking by email.

Sunday is a day of rest

Checkout pauses on Sundays, because we keep Sunday as a day of rest. Your bag stays saved, so you can check out on Monday.

Independent, and proud of it

Rent Coat Co. is made by a member, for members. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Scripture references link to the Church's own website so you can read the full chapter.
