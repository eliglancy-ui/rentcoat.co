The name

In Alma 46 in the Book of Mormon, the Nephites are close to losing their freedom. Captain Moroni, the leader of their armies, tears his own coat, writes a reminder of what they're defending on one piece, and fastens it to the end of a pole. He calls it the title of liberty, and he goes out among the people waving it so everyone can see what he wrote.

“He rent his coat; and he took a piece thereof, and wrote upon it—In memory of our God, our religion, and freedom, and our peace, our wives, and our children.”

Alma 46:12

"Rent" is the old word for torn. Rent Coat Co. is named for that moment: take what you have and turn it into something you stand behind. The flag in our logo is the title of liberty. We make shirts that look like a real streetwear drop and still say what you believe.

The shirts

Every Rent Coat Co. tee is a Shaka Wear SHGDD: 7.5 oz heavyweight cotton, garment-dyed for a broken-in feel, with a boxy, oversized, drop-shoulder fit in S to 2XL. Each design has a scripture reference on it, and each shirt is printed for you after the drop closes.

How drops work

We sell in short, limited drops instead of keeping a warehouse full of shirts. Each drop is a 7-day preorder: you order while the countdown is running, and when it hits zero that drop is done. Every shirt is printed after the drop closes, so nothing goes to waste, and orders arrive about 2 to 3 weeks later with tracking by email.

Checkout pauses on Sundays while a drop is open, because we keep Sunday as a day of rest. Your bag stays saved, so you can check out on Monday.

Independent, and proud of it

Rent Coat Co. is made by a member, for members. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Scripture references link to the Church's own website so you can read the full chapter.
