Timing

When to order.

Launch week runs from Saturday, November 14 at 10:00 AM Eastern to Saturday, November 21 at 10:00 AM Eastern, with every tee at $35. Launch week orders are printed after it ends and are expected to arrive about December 5 to 12.

After launch week the shirts stay in the shop at $40. Those orders are printed right away and usually arrive in 1 to 2 weeks, so order by early December for the best chance before Christmas. These are estimates, not guarantees.

If it won't arrive in time, print our gift card: fill in their name and the shirt, and put it under the tree. The shirt follows.

Not sure of their size? The fit is boxy and oversized, so their usual size gives the intended look. Shipping is US only.

Shopping for a missionary who's already serving? Most missionaries wear casual clothes only on preparation day, so a shirt usually makes a better gift before they leave or for when they come home.

Questions

FAQ

What's a good Christmas gift for a Latter-day Saint teen?

Something they'll wear to school, not just to church. Rent Coat Co. tees are heavyweight, boxy streetwear with a Book of Mormon story on the back: the Title of Liberty, Stripling 2000, or Cureloms & Cumoms. They're $35 during launch week (Nov 14–21) and $40 after.

Will it arrive by Christmas?

Launch week orders (Nov 14–21) are expected to arrive about December 5 to 12. Orders after launch week usually arrive in 1 to 2 weeks, so order by early December for the best chance. Delivery by Christmas isn't guaranteed. You can print a gift card to give in the meantime. If a shirt can't ship by the date we've stated, we'll email you a new date and you can cancel for a full refund.

What's a good gift for a missionary?

Stripling 2000 is our pick for someone about to leave on a mission. Missionaries mostly wear casual clothes only on preparation day, so it works best before they leave or after they come home.

What size should I get as a gift?

Their usual size. The fit is boxy and oversized, with dropped shoulders. Sizes are unisex S to 2XL, and a medium is about 24¾ inches across the chest and 27¼ inches long. Made-to-order shirts can't be returned for fit.

How much is it, and when can I order?

$35 ($38 for 2XL) during Drop 001 launch week, from Saturday, November 14, 2026 at 10:00 AM Eastern to Saturday, November 21 at 10:00 AM Eastern. After that it's $40 ($43 for 2XL) and stays in the shop. Shipping is a flat $5.99 to US addresses, and checkout pauses on Sunday. Launch week orders are printed after launch week ends and usually arrive 2 to 3 weeks later; orders after that usually arrive in 1 to 2 weeks.
