Where the name comes from

The banner and the name of Christ.

Our flagship shirt is the title of liberty, the banner Captain Moroni made from his torn coat in Alma 46. In the same moment, he prayed for liberty to rest on his people “so long as there should a band of Christians remain to possess the land” (Alma 46:13).

“All those who were true believers in Christ took upon them, gladly, the name of Christ, or Christians as they were called.”Alma 46:15

That's the idea behind every Rent Coat Co. drop: clothes that look like they came from a normal streetwear brand, and still say what you believe.

How we make it

Built like streetwear.

Faith shirts usually mean a slogan on a thin tee. We start from the blank: a 7.5 oz garment-dyed Shaka Wear in a boxy, drop-shoulder cut, with a big back print and a small chest mark.

Launch weeks

New designs launch with a 7-day launch week at a lower price, then stay in the shop.

Printed to order

Every shirt is printed after you order, so there's no warehouse of leftovers.

Sunday is a day of rest

Checkout pauses on Sundays while a drop is open. Your bag stays saved for Monday.

Questions

FAQ

Is Rent Coat Co. a Christian clothing brand?

Yes. It's an independent faith streetwear brand made by a member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Every design starts with a reference from the Book of Mormon, which members read alongside the Bible as scripture that testifies of Jesus Christ.

Do I have to be a Latter-day Saint to wear it?

No. The shirts are made with young Latter-day Saints in mind, but anyone who likes them is welcome. The story behind each one is on its page.

What makes it streetwear and not a church shirt?

The blank and the drop. It's a 7.5 oz garment-dyed tee in a boxy, drop-shoulder fit, with a big back print and a small chest mark, with new designs launching in a 7-day launch week.

Is Rent Coat Co. affiliated with the Church?

No. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

How much is it, and when can I order?

After that it's $40 ($43 for 2XL) and stays in the shop. Shipping is a flat $5.99 to US addresses, and checkout pauses on Sunday. Launch week orders are printed after launch week ends and usually arrive 2 to 3 weeks later; orders after that usually arrive in 1 to 2 weeks.
